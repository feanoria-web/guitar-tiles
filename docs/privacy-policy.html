Riffline — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Riffline ("the app") is developed by Feanorian ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

Summary

  • Riffline works fully offline and does not request internet access.
  • We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, account information, or device identifiers.
  • The app contains no ads, no analytics, and no crash-reporting SDKs.

Song files you import

When you import a song file, Riffline uses Android's standard file picker (Storage Access Framework). You choose the file directly; the app only reads the file you select and copies it into its own private app storage on your device. Riffline never scans your device's storage, never requests broad storage or media permissions, and never uploads imported files anywhere.

Scores and progress

Your scores, high scores, and settings are saved in a file on your device only. This data is never sent off your device and is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app.

Permissions

Riffline does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, network, or install-unknown-apps permissions. The app only needs local storage access (through the file picker described above) to import songs.

External links

The song browser menu includes optional links to third-party community sites (such as enchor.us and rhythmverse.co) where you can find additional song files. These links open in your device's browser. Riffline does not share any data with these sites, and your use of them is governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.

Children's privacy

Riffline does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data from any user.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date.
